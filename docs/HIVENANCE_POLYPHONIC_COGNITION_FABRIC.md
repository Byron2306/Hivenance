# HiveNance Polyphonic Cognition Fabric

**Status:** canonical architecture specification  
**Scope:** Phoenix relative-value research organism  
**Authority:** research only; no live execution or promotion authority

## 1. Purpose

HiveNance is not a voting swarm of indicators.

It is a distributed market-research cognition fabric in which independent evidence families observe the same bound world state, communicate hypotheses, recruit or challenge one another, propagate urgent changes, form multi-timescale resonance, face adversarial falsification, account for cognitive expenditure, and produce governed research conclusions.

Phoenix is the first market-research organism running on this fabric.

## 2. Constitutional separation

The architecture preserves six hard separations:

1. **Observation is not interpretation.**
2. **Correlation is not causation.**
3. **Resonance is not truth.**
4. **Confidence is not authority.**
5. **Research support is not execution permission.**
6. **Synthetic evidence is not prospective market proof.**

## 7. Waggle Protocol

The Waggle Protocol is the colony's typed hypothesis language.

Canonical messages:

- `WAGGLE` — bounded hypothesis proposal;
- `FOLLOW` — independent corroboration;
- `DISSENT` — independent contradiction;
- `SEARCH` — request targeted evidence;
- `ABANDON` — withdraw prior support;
- `ALARM` — prerequisite or relationship failure;
- `SETTLE` — future outcome is now observable.

A Waggle receipt should bind:

- world_state_id;
- pair / asset / market scope;
- horizon;
- hypothesis class;
- direction or abstain;
- expected magnitude if available;
- uncertainty;
- evidence family;
- lineage digest;
- evidence root;
- authority.

## 7A. Law of Harmony

All bee testimony must pass a deterministic constitutional validator before it may enter the choir.

The Law of Harmony v1 enforces:

1. **World-state fidelity** — testimony must bind to the current world-state id/hash and freshness window.
2. **Lineage honesty** — independence is earned only by a registered lineage; clones cannot manufacture quorum.
3. **Horizon polyphony** — micro, meso and macro voices remain distinct; a global direction may not be collapsed across bands.
4. **Dissent preservation** — valid DISSENT remains visible and may not be silently discarded.
5. **Authority conservation** — testimony, resonance, harmony and positive pulses cannot create execution or promotion authority.
6. **Asymmetric safety** — SEARCH/DISCOVERY/CLEAR pulses may increase attention only; ALARM/FREEZE may reduce or freeze authority.
7. **Evidence binding** — admitted testimony must carry an evidence root.
8. **Fail-closed staleness** — stale, future-skewed or world-drifted testimony is refused.

The Harmony Law answers:

> Is this bee allowed to sing this testimony into this choir now?

It does not answer:

> Is the hypothesis correct?

Nor:

> May Phoenix trade?

`strategies/relative_value_lab/harmony_law.py`

Its receipts remain bound to:

`research_evidence_only_no_execution_or_promotion_authority`

## 8. Musical cognition — motif, rhythm and cadence

HiveNance does **not** model a hypothesis as a rigid lifecycle or workflow state
machine.

A market idea is represented as a **motif**: a recurring bounded pattern carried
by one or more independent voices through time.

The cognition fabric listens for musical structure:

- **note** — one evidence-bound observation or testimony;
- **voice** — one independent evidentiary lineage;
- **motif** — a recognizable hypothesis pattern recurring through time;
- **phrase** — a bounded temporal development of a motif;
- **call** — a voice introduces or re-introduces a motif;
- **response** — another voice answers it independently;
- **counterpoint** — distinct voices carry compatible but non-identical lines;
- **dissonance** — meaningful unresolved disagreement;
- **consonance** — locally coherent relationship among voices;
- **pulse** — urgency / accent, not authority;
- **tempo** — rate at which meaningful evidence arrives;
- **rhythm** — temporal spacing and recurrence structure;
- **cadence** — tendency toward local resolution;
- **syncopation** — informative off-pattern timing;
- **rest** — intentional abstention / absence of sufficient evidence;
- **crescendo** — increasing independent evidentiary pressure;
- **decrescendo** — fading support;
- **modulation** — change of regime, horizon or relationship context;
- **resolution** — a motif becomes prospectively settleable or is resolved by
  later evidence;
- **coda** — closed evidentiary phrase retained for Nurse / Crystal learning.

There is no mandatory sequence such as FORMING → CORROBORATED → CHALLENGED.
A motif may:

```text
enter softly
→ be answered in counterpoint
→ become dissonant
→ rest
→ return in a different key / regime
→ crescendo
→ cadence
→ resolve
```

or it may simply fade without resolution.

The accumulator therefore tracks continuous musical features rather than
categorical belief states:

- motif recurrence;
- voice diversity;
- phrase duration;
- inter-onset intervals;
- tempo and tempo drift;
- rhythmic regularity;
- syncopation;
- call / response latency;
- counterpoint diversity;
- consonance and dissonance;
- tension trajectory;
- dynamic intensity;
- crescendo / decrescendo;
- modulation events;
- rest density;
- cadence strength;
- resolution evidence.

**Silence is evidence.** A missing response, a long rest, or a motif that fails to
recur can be as informative as another supporting note.

**Dissonance is not failure.** It is preserved as musical tension and may be more
informative than premature consensus.

**Cadence is not authority.** A strong cadence may make a motif eligible for a
prospective research transition, but cannot create execution or promotion
authority.

The Law of Harmony remains constitutional: it determines whether a voice may
enter the score. Musical cognition determines how lawful voices relate through
time.

## 9. Market Hunting

Metatron's threat-hunting engine proactively searches telemetry for defined patterns.

Phoenix adapts this into proactive market hypothesis hunting.

A hunt is not a forecast and not an action. It is a bounded question such as:

- extreme relative displacement + flow exhaustion;
- depth depletion followed by recovery;
- relative spread excursion while relationship stability remains high;
- structural-break candidate;
- cross-pair asset shock;
- forecast-family disagreement spike;
- low-cost direct-route anomaly;
- context-burn anomaly;
- pulse cascade without independent evidence.

Each hunt must declare:

- required data sources;
- deterministic detection logic;
- falsification condition;
- scope;
- severity / research priority;
- false-positive notes;
- output message type, normally SEARCH or ALARM.

## 11. Causal Cascade

Correlation asks whether observations are related.

Cascade asks whether an evidence-supported propagation path exists.

Example:

```text
aggressor sell burst
→ book depletion
→ relative spread excursion
→ quote OFI turn
→ depth recovery
→ reversal forecast
```

Cascade scope may expand:

```text
PAIR → CONNECTED PAIRS → ASSET → MARKET
```

Every edge must carry evidence and timing.

## 15. Mystique counterfactual maze

Metatron Mystique builds coherent mirror worlds to exhaust adversarial reasoning.

Phoenix uses explicitly synthetic counterfactual worlds to attack its own hypotheses.

Examples:

- remove the largest trade;
- remove depth recovery;
- invert flow;
- double spread cost;
- break relationship stability;
- remove one evidence lineage;
- change Horizon regime;
- perturb timing within measured uncertainty.

Mystique output is a falsification receipt:

- worlds tested;
- worlds survived;
- failure modes;
- evidence dependencies;
- fragility score.

Synthetic worlds are never mixed with prospective market evidence.

## 16. Cognitive metabolism

Adapted from Metatron CBR / TBCR / CDI.

Phoenix measures:

### Research Context Burn Rate

Evidence or observation budget consumed per new useful settled information unit.

### Tool Budget Consumption Rate

Model / tool evaluations per useful settlement.

### Confidence Degradation Index

Relative decline between hypothesis confidence at formation and confidence after settlement.

The metabolism layer controls research attention.

It cannot grant execution authority.

High burn with low information gain should lead toward ABANDON.

## 17. Learned Challenger / ML

Metatron contains network anomaly, classification, time-series and ensemble ML.

Phoenix ML is a challenger family, not an oracle.

Permitted roles:

- anomaly detection;
- event classification;
- probability-of-threshold-crossing models;
- flow-exhaustion classification;
- regime classification;
- learned forecast challenger;
- calibrated ensemble challenger.

Requirements:

- time-ordered training;
- outcome-lag protection;
- explicit train / validation provenance;
- baseline comparison;
- calibration;
- leave-one-pair / leave-one-asset diagnostics;
- dependence-aware uncertainty;
- versioned model lineage;
- no same-window tuning presented as validation.

Synthetic training may test software behavior only.

It cannot establish edge or promotion evidence.
