## Critical distinctions

1. TemporalTexture is NOT Temporal Participation.
TemporalTexture measures the cadence of evidence/messages. Temporal Participation will measure the market's 24-hour participation rhythm and activity relative to the normal level for that UTC interval.

2. PolyphonicQuorum is NOT Council consensus.
Council aggregates strategy proposals. PolyphonicQuorum verifies independently rooted, temporally coordinated research choreography while preserving dissent.

3. ConductingQueen is NOT legacy GovernanceQueen.
The former conducts score, notation, triune cognition and research epoch. The latter scores actionable worker proposals and remains a legacy/proposal adapter.

4. VNS/Mystique counterfactuals are NOT observed historical controls.
Synthetic challenge worlds remain contamination-separated. The new Comparison Engine adds observed matched historical comparisons and controls.

5. Evidence Bees are NOT Workers.
Bees describe bounded evidence families. Workers propose strategies/hypotheses from evidence.
