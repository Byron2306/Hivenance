## S0.6 root-lineage independence policy

The Hive must distinguish three concepts:

1. DIFFERENT TRANSFORMATION: two nodes compute different functions.
2. DIFFERENT LINEAGE: two nodes come from separately governed organs.
3. INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE ROOT: the underlying observations are materially independent.

Only #3 may increase independent-evidence quorum weight.

Examples:
- 1h return, trend score and realized volatility from the same candles are different transformations, not independent roots.
- Horizon trend and Edge trend may be different lineages but share the same candle root.
- order-book depth and candle return may be independent evidence roots if captured separately and timestamp-bound.
- a learned model trained on the same features is a challenger, not a new independent witness.
- synthetic Mystique worlds never count as observed evidence roots.

Every future WorldGraph node must declare:
- source_kind;
- source_ids / evidence roots;
- lineage id;
- transformation id/version;
- observed_at / available_at;
- freshness;
- synthetic flag;
- authority.
